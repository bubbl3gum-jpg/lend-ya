## Deliberate differences from the reference

The design study is a focused proof, not a pixel-for-pixel recreation or replacement homepage. It does not add the reference's map band, favorites, notification delivery, fabricated ratings or feature claims. The existing browse/station/booking flows remain linked and authoritative; MRT line distinctions and verification data are untouched. Eligible category names/listing records come from the actual database. With no eligible records it shows honest empty states. The screenshot fixture products use original engraved illustrations and are explicitly synthetic; real products retain their own persisted media.

Original generated atmospheric artwork and object plates were made for this candidate, not copied from proprietary reference photography/illustrations. Asset rights/provenance limitations are documented in [ASSETS.md](ASSETS.md). Product-owner approval and wider release are separate steps.
