# Recovery proposal and Stage 9 assessment

Password recovery remains **unimplemented**. Existing forgot-password intake has no real delivery; no reset token, email link, identity reassociation, recovery bypass or notification was added. Password change requires a current persisted session and knowledge of the existing password; it cannot serve a locked-out user.

Separate approval must define enumeration-safe intake, real delivery provider/operational ownership, cryptographic random hashed single-use purpose/account-bound expiring tokens, strict Origin/CSRF and rate limits, atomic token consumption, concurrency/replay protection, password validation using existing hashing, all-session/assurance invalidation and minimal immutable events. Future reset/change writers must share the credential serialization contract with verified login issuance. Delivery needs a durable outbox/retry/monitoring policy, honest enqueue vs delivery status, and user notifications without exposing account existence or credentials.

Staff/passkey loss and verified-human association recovery remain separate independently reviewed human workflows. Never infer privileged identity recovery from ordinary email access or a password reset screen. No password-only, email-only, self-approved privileged recovery or automatic Admin/role promotion. Existing rental, return, inspection, dispute and Support obligations must remain accessible under their current guards.
