# Assisted passkey recovery refinement — Batch 5C

24 October 2026 (Asia/Taipei). Refines `ASSISTED_PASSKEY_RECOVERY_PROPOSAL_BATCH_5B.md`; both remain **proposals, not approved recovery implementation**. No provider, recovery eligibility rule, reset credential, emergency bypass or mandatory MFA is enabled.

## Distinct responsibilities, not an automatic four-person rule

Support investigates and recommends, without identity-proofing authority or reset rights. Trusted identity re-verification is performed by an explicitly approved qualified reviewer using approved private evidence and canonical alias reconciliation. Independent human approval evaluates the exact recovery request, conflicts, findings, affected accounts/keys/sessions and policy. A narrowly scoped technical executor applies an already authorized intent; execution is **not inherently another human approval**.

| Feasible staffing model | Separation that must hold | Unresolved approval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two qualified humans plus constrained machine executor | Human A investigates/recommends; distinct Human B independently re-verifies identity and approves if qualified and unconflicted. Machine checks the exact signed/bound decision and performs only the authorized atomic transition. Claimant/aliases count as neither reviewer. | Organization must approve whether combined re-verification/approval is acceptable for the incident risk and whether A may combine investigation/sponsorship. No machine identity/credential is created now. |
| Three humans plus constrained executor | Human A investigates, Human B independently re-verifies identity, Human C approves. Execution may be automated or mechanically carried out by an authorized human without becoming an additional approval. | Approve qualification, conflicts and whether the executor may also be C; no ability to revise the approved intent while executing. |
| Small team with independent external reviewer | Use a previously approved external identity/security reviewer to supply independence where staff are unavailable. Access is narrowly scoped and audited. | Approve provider/authority/confidentiality and reviewer identity; do not invent one. |
| Only one eligible reviewer available | Recovery waits/escalates; the claimant, another account alias or a technical operator cannot substitute for an independent approver. | No implicit emergency exception. Mandatory MFA must wait for operational reviewer coverage. |

High-risk approval always involves distinct authorized humans as required by policy. A machine executor verifies authorization and cannot manufacture approval, re-verify human identity or approve its own reset. No autonomous AI/service principal receives human approval rights. Bootstrap and small-team staffing shortages are blockers to resolve openly, not reasons for ADMIN flags or hidden accounts.

## Proposed end-to-end transaction and handoff

1. Open a private documented incident. Support records loss versus suspected compromise, affected canonical human/accounts, remaining keys, session exposure and rental/support obligations. Password/email access alone cannot establish identity. Preserve the original case and an immutable recommendation reference.
2. Independently re-verify the human under the approved authority/evidence policy and reconcile aliases. Audit proof access without copying raw proof into application/general audit logs. A changed email or another account is not independent evidence.
3. An eligible independent human approves the **exact** versioned recovery intent: case, canonical identity, impacted credentials/sessions, invalidation scope, replacement-enrollment conditions, expiry, reason and notification plan. Participant/investigator/reviewer conflicts and stale policy/epochs deny. Changes require a new decision. Eligibility, delay and escalation conditions remain explicit product/security decisions.
4. A future constrained executor rechecks approval, identity, session, versions, scope/conflicts and incident status inside the reviewed transaction boundary. Atomically mark old credentials revoked; advance factor/authorization invalidation; invalidate affected staff bindings and AuthSessions; and record durable immutable decision/execution history. Include every relevant account alias. No destructive deletion of staff/audit history. Design exact grant/membership consequences for loss-only versus compromise; do not automatically reactivate a membership.
5. Atomically persist the bounded replacement-enrollment handoff and notification outbox alongside invalidation/audit. After a valid recovery decision, old passwords/sessions alone must not redeem it. Require independently approved session reauthentication and single-use, short-lived, exact-person/account/case/origin enrollment authorization. Then complete actual WebAuthn registration **and signed assertion**. Consumption/audit failure must not release a credential/assurance outside the authorized transition. No such handoff or reset endpoint exists now.
6. Notify previously verified channels for request/approval/execution/rejection and replacement completion, with no bearer secrets/proof bodies. Dispatch from a reviewed durable outbox after commit; provider failure must retry and alert. External delivery cannot be rolled back atomically with PostgreSQL. Define what lack of delivery blocks versus what is an alert, and handle ambiguous commit/idempotency explicitly rather than claiming distributed exactly-once delivery.
7. Preserve old credential/session/decision attribution with approved private evidence retention. Resolve a failed replacement without reviving old keys or resetting epochs. Preserve ordinary custody/return/inspection/support access through separate account reauthentication; recovery never removes these obligations or expands staff access.

## No execution until decisions are approved

Required decisions: identity authority/evidence, reviewer qualifications and combined-duty eligibility, independent-human capacity, lost-versus-compromised incident rules, notification channels/delivery thresholds, recovery delay/expiry, reset/session/membership scope, handoff authentication, evidence retention and emergency procedure. No live recovery implementation is authorized by this refinement.

There is no password-only/email-only reset, fallback TOTP, manual flag, hidden ADMIN, SQL bypass or emergency MFA override. An emergency can escalate to an approved qualified independent reviewer; it cannot erase independence. Any future break-glass design requires a separately approved custody/authorization/audit/review policy before deployment or mandatory enforcement.
