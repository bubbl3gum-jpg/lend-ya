## Complexity and next contracts

Implemented read model/window/private-reference + owner/borrower non-submitting forms: medium, no schema/migration. Actual structured claim/response persistence: medium/high, requires additive immutable participant events, optimistic/idempotent writes, reference bindings and atomic audit; reconcile legacy dispute status without touching original return. Reliable notification/delivery/start and exceptions: high operational/security policy dependency. Vision/eKYC are contracts only, not functional services. Full recovery, financial handling and identity processing remain separately unapproved.

No synthetic “delivered” marker is accepted from the browser. No late claim is implicitly allowed by a preview, and no global production deadline enforcement is claimed. Legacy production free-text dispute behavior is preserved; it remains distinct from the proposed standard window until reviewed cutover.
