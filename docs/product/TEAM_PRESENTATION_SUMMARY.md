# Hustlen’ — team presentation summary

2 November 2026. Hustlen’ lets someone lend a physical item and someone else request a rental at an agreed MRT meeting station. It keeps track of who must act next and retains pickup/return evidence rather than treating a QR scan or chat message as proof that custody changed.

The development product contains accounts, item listings, browsing, booking approval, MRT selection, bilateral pickup/return, inspection, reports/disputes, reviews and an authorized Admin workspace. Selected pages use the heritage agricultural-catalogue appearance and English/Traditional Chinese controls. Listing prices/deposits are displayed in USD; no payments or deposit deductions are processed.

Consolidation preserves the newer work and reconciles the existing MRT/main history without importing another map. List an item and My rentals are now easier to find. Help explains planning, pickup/return/inspection, reports and the limits of experimental AI. Existing private Support/staff/announcement/settings pilots remain isolated rather than appearing live.

Local Qwen is a separate, fictional-only Customer Service experiment. It can produce structured suggestions, uncertainties and unsent drafts. It cannot examine photographs, retrieve customers’ private records, refund money or decide responsibility. Genuine model quality and the Mac presentation must still be checked; mocked tests do not prove Qwen quality. Vision and other future providers are not activated here.

Use DEMO_RUNBOOK.md for the six scenes and safe startup/shutdown. COMPLETE_TEST_REPORT.md contains fresh evidence and failures; REMAINING_ISSUES.md and RELEASE_READINESS_REPORT.md govern the decision. A working development rehearsal is not production-launch approval. The next milestone is closing remaining demonstrated defects and owner/device acceptance, not adding more features.
