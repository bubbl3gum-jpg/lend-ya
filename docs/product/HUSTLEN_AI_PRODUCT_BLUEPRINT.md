# Hustlen’ — Canonical AI Product Blueprint

Version 0.3 · agreed product requirements for planning and bounded development · 28 October 2026, Asia/Taipei. **Not final production policy or activation approval.** This document adopts the owner's Batch 13B decisions and supersedes the working v0.1 only as current product direction; [original v0.1](../ai/AI_PRODUCT_TRUST_SYSTEM_BLUEPRINT_V0_1.md) is preserved historically. The [canonical fourteen-stage roadmap](../roadmap/MASTER_ROADMAP_14_STAGES.md) remains authoritative for stage names/order/status. Engineering batches are increments, not new master stages.

## Agreed experience and authority

**Customer Service:** explain procedures/troubleshoot routine problems; assist parties in proposing mutually acceptable recovery; organize permitted evidence for independently authorized human staff. No automatic guilt, penalty, refund or dispute-resolution decision. Private participant-to-Support and internal staff records remain isolated from any shared coordination space.

**Owner:** Hybrid Listing Studio, starting from photographs or manual facts. Assist writing, details, missing information, declared accessories and condition documentation. Owner confirms material facts, pricing/availability and explicitly publishes. No inferred authenticity, condition or automatic publication.

**Borrower:** hybrid search-first experience. Simple searches return real eligible listings; complex requests may receive comparison, recommendations and optional conversation. Never fabricate listing details, compatibility or availability. Discovery assistance does not create bookings.

**eKYC:** registration, sign-in, browsing and exploration remain available without verification. Successful identity verification is required before publishing or submitting a rental request in the intended product. Initial supported Taiwan National ID and ARC document types; country expansion via controlled registry. Exact document versions/authenticity, provider, lawful biometric processing, review, retention and production eligibility activation remain unapproved. Batch 13C clarifies the agreed assurance requirement: document authenticity, live face/liveness verification and a valid face-to-document match must all be established before authoritative eligibility. Its owned-development simulation does not perform any of these real checks. Existing boolean flags are not provider verification. Do not activate a fake verified state or impair ongoing return/inspection/Support obligations.

**Event verification:** approved policy may request randomized live face/liveness or supplemental item capture at pickup/return. Never continuous biometric surveillance during a rental. Challenges supplement essential evidence; alternatives and exceptions require separate approval. No service activated by this blueprint.

**Vision:** hybrid capture. Essential evidence remains required; poor quality prompts retake; minor issues may allow acknowledged continuation only where approved; unavailable essentials require controlled exception. Possible differences are advisory with references/uncertainty and human review. No automatic damage causation, valuation, liability or deposit action. No live provider or external image transmission approved.

**Listing documentation:** before publication require basic photos, declared accessories, known condition/defects and owner confirmation. Pickup and return require fresh comprehensive evidence, relevant condition/accessory checks and all existing verification requirements. Supplemental randomized views remain future separately approved work. Listing advertised condition is not an immutable rental pickup baseline; original rental records must be protected.

## Agreed inspection and response durations

- Owner inspection window: **24 elapsed hours after authoritative verified physical return**. No deadline reset on page load. A borrower submission or scan alone is insufficient.
- Borrower response window: **48 elapsed hours after a qualifying authoritative dispute notification event**. Composed/queued messages do not establish delivery. Reliable delivery semantics, failure handling and deadline exceptions remain open before production activation.
- Silence is never guilt/admission. Expiry never authorizes an AI charge or deposit deduction.
- Physical custody, inspection, dispute and future financial settlement are distinct. A recorded return remains returned while condition/commercial issues are open.

Batch 13B's owned-development candidate uses only the existing completed return QR receipt plus explicit physical acknowledgment. It displays authoritative records and validates unsent drafts; it does not impose these deadlines on production legacy writers. There is no qualifying real notification-delivery service, so enforceable response deadlines stay disabled. Synthetic notification examples are test fixtures, never delivery proof.

## Meetup, recovery and design

Location sharing/ETA is optional, consented and time-bounded meetup coordination, not attendance proof. Scheduling changes require same-proposal acceptance by both participants plus deterministic permission/booking checks. AI suggests alternatives but does not execute them. Secure lockers are future work requiring independent custody/access/verification/liability design. No Maps billing, tracking or production geofencing changes follow.

Premium vintage agricultural catalogue brought to life: inherit current heritage materials, serif/supporting typography, readable controls, quiet rules, accessibility and motion preferences. No sensory effects in consequential evidence/security workflows. Stage 9 aesthetic acceptance remains pending; this does not approve a whole-site redesign.
