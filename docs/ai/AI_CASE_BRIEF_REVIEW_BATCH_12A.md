## Functional demonstration

Its research notebook displays four authored bilingual cases with source context, scripted summary/category suggestions, missing/conflicting information, follow-up questions and editable local reply drafts. Keyboard citations focus exact sources; review requires human acknowledgment, edits clear review, language switching preserves drafts and their language, and switching/reopening confirms before discarding edits. **Every sample output is explicitly scripted, not model inference.
