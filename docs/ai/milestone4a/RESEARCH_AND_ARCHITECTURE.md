Use a **separate post-validation translation sidecar**, not translation inside Qwen reasoning and not a translated replacement for canonical evidence. Existing authored UI catalogues remain authoritative for interface text. Keep Qwen's current bilingual drafts and original response available. Initial future scope: user-requested translation of an allowlisted subset of an already validated **synthetic** Advisory. No arbitrary-text endpoint, raw conversations, source-record retrieval, automatic handoff or background translation.

Shortlist **Google Cloud Translation Advanced and Azure AI Translator** for an eventual small EN↔Taiwan Traditional Chinese trial, subject to current language-pair/glossary/privacy/region verification and owner selection. **DeepL remains a conditional candidate**: its official specification includes zh-Hant in some resources, but that is not sufficient to claim the required text translation/glossary pair is enabled for the chosen account/model. Do not select a provider from reputation or a fluent demo. No measured Taiwan rental-domain quality/latency evidence exists here.

Do not add a third local model to the owner's M4/24GB Mac without feasibility and license review: Customer Service Qwen and provisional Vision already have pending acceptance/resource constraints. A local translation baseline is an option, not a zero-cost or verified-compatible default.

These are proposed glossary/style targets, not always context-free replacements. Do not globally substitute words across quoted sources, names, negated statements or legal meaning.

Report actual future translation-only and total AI+translation latency separately, warm/cold, failures and rate-limiting; do not add an invented SLA.
